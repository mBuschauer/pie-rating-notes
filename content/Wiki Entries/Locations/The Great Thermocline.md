- [[Wiki Entries/Spirits and Gods/Greater Spirits/Leviathan|Leviathan]] travels in more or less a straight line across the center of [[Wiki Entries/Locations/Aecor|Aecor]] from West to East every day, bringing with it the movements of the tides and waves. 
- Midway through its journey, it comes face to face with [[Wiki Entries/Spirits and Gods/Greater Spirits/Phoenix|Phoenix]], who travels from East to West every day, guiding the path of the Sun to bring life-giving warmth and light for all. 
- Their overlapping paths define the Great Thermocline, a small but entirely uninhabitable section of the world that is constantly burning, boiling, steaming, and flooding in an endless, daily cycle. 
- If your home island is unfortunate enough to drift too close to the Thermocline, it is a near guarantee that it will have to be vacated for at least a year. 
- Trying to cross the Thermocline during the day is a task only very well-prepared and experienced sailors should take on. 
- [[Wiki Entries/Spirits and Gods/Greater Spirits/Phoenix|Phoenix]] and [[Wiki Entries/Spirits and Gods/Greater Spirits/Leviathan|Leviathan]] are said to make the journey beneath the world every night to return to their proper starting places each morning.
