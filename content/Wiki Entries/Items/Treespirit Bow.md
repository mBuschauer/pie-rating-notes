*Weapon (Shortbow), (Requires Attunement)*

An old-fashioned and rugged shortbow made with the wood and bark of a great tree, gifted freely to the craftsperson who constructed it. 

- You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.
- The wielder can cast Thorn Whip at will without material components.
- Once per long rest, the wielder can cast Entangle without expending a spell slot.
