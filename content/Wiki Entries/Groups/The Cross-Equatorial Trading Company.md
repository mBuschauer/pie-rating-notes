- The richest, most prolific merchant organization in the East. 
- They are well-known for their skilled and reliable captains and crews, who are experts at evading pirates and generally defending passengers and cargo. 
- They have agreements with several independent states across [[Wiki Entries/Locations/Aecor|Aecor]], and have exclusive contracts with some for the most premier locations in the East:
	- [[Leisure City]], the largest floating structure in the world
	- [[Ralestal]], the largest city in the East and the location of the last [[Arcane Academy]]






- The Cross-Equatorial Trading Company was founded about a century ago by the wealthy Joracen family, who, at the time, were the royal house of [[Ralestal]], the Arcane City and the Jewel of the East.
- At their founding, they used advanced magic to assist in evading and otherwise addressing the many dangers that were becoming more and more obtrusive to long-distance trade. 
- They were the first group in recorded history to document the [[Wiki Entries/Spirits and Gods/Greater Spirits/Phoenix|Phoenix]]'s entire flight path, especially how large an area becomes immediately dangerous due to its proximity to the elemental, and describing several of its behavioral patterns. 
- CETC captains are still broadly considered the only ones you can trust to make it across  [[Wiki Entries/Locations/The Great Thermocline|The Great Thermocline]] during the day without being burned or boiled alive. 
- The Joracens still own the company to this day and the current head, Eliza Joracen, is a shrewd and observant [[Firbolg]] woman who has led the company into its most successful era, albeit its most tumultuous as well.
- Initially, the CETC more or less bought out ships and crews they scouted out and deemed trustworthy to transport cargo and their officials. 
	- They supply mages and general necessities, deal with local governments, organize trade agreements, manage shipping routes, cover unforeseen costs, and much more. 
	- They take the payment for any shipment one of their vessels makes and distribute that payment through a robust, tiered salary system across the entire company. 
	- They also own a pretty substantial amount of productive land of all kinds, including mines and farms, whose products they can then distribute using the infrastructure they have already put in place. 
- Their upper brass are composed of regional and project directors, most of which have been at the company for years. 
- The CETC is more or less entirely profit-driven but they do have some moral boundaries. They don’t believe in committing plain theft and at least try to avoid violence whenever it makes sense for them.
- They support and follow most local laws, but are quick to change them if the officials that make said laws have a price tag they can manage or will buckle under some well-placed pressure.
- Joining the CETC in basically any position requires a decently high reputation and the skills to back it up. The pay is high, they offer actual job security and avoid taking potentially lethal risks, and you are less likely to get into legal trouble anywhere in the world just by being associated with them. 
- While in their founding years they reached out to captains and crews, nowadays captains and crews ask them for jobs. The disadvantage of their exponential growth over the last several decades is that the mages that were once one of their major selling points are spread quite thin across their fleet, leaving ships somewhat more vulnerable to attacks from pirates and monsters. They operate almost exclusively in the Northeast and Southeast quadrants of the world, simply because that’s where the money is. They have long since moved their base of operations from [[Ralestal]] in the East-Northeast to[[ Port Amicitia]] in the South-Southeast. The decision was considered questionable a few decades ago when it was first made, but [[Ralestal]] was conquered by a Northern warlord somewhat recently, so perhaps they had a bit of foresight or maybe just good luck.
