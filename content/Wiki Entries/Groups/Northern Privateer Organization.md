- Sometime in the past century, a bunch of fed-up soldiers, pirates, ordinary townsfolk, all victims of the endless conflicts between the warlords, decided to form a group of warriors and sailors that devoted their lives to protecting the citizens of the far Northeast. 
- After a few brutal decades for the organization, it seems they have instead decided that their services require payment. 
- After that decision, you’ve seen as they fell into relative obscurity.
