It is quite uncommon to see Southern refugees this far North, but as of late there have been a few groups of impoverished and visibly traumatized people arriving via trade ships. They all originated from a small archipelago in the far South that was seemingly a protectorate of some kind. You don’t recall who or what was their supposed protector. The ones who fled this far felt that because their islands were devastated by what they called some sort of shadowy demon-monster, they couldn’t rely on the people who promised to defend them when they didn’t manage to act quick enough.
