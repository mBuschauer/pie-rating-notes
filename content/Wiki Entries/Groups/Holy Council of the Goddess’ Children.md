- Perhaps the largest single nation in the world. The Holy Council is a theocratic country based in the West-southwest of [[Wiki Entries/Locations/Aecor|Aecor]] and reportedly has been attempting an expansion northward but has been met with a lot of armed resistance. 
- Your main point of contact with them has likely been glancing at a bounty board by chance and seeing that at least half of the bounties were issued by the Holy Council. 
- They obviously have a lot of money and influence to throw around, but you don’t know if you’ve ever seen a member up here. 
- They are apparently monotheistic and believe in [[Wiki Entries/Spirits and Gods/God|God]]. 
	- They view [[Wiki Entries/Spirits and Gods/Greater Spirits/The Sun|The Sun]] as a near-omnipotent singular Goddess of Life, the Land, and the Sky.
