- You probably haven’t met all that many actual wizards in your life, and’ve probably met about as many [[Wiki Entries/Items/Isothos|Isotho]] wielders. 
- These folks tend to stand out. Fine cloak, hood always on, at least one [[Wiki Entries/Items/Isothos|Isotho]], a locked spellbook, an open or closed eye symbol on their outfit somewhere. 
- They roll into town and disappear the next day. They’re always very kind if you talk to them, and from what you’ve heard they are primarily interested in research. 
- The main reason they get dirty looks from most people, however, are the rumors that they mutilate themselves to replace their organs and limbs with more of those [[Wiki Entries/History/Ancient Civilizations/Arlun|Arlunite]] prosthetics.
