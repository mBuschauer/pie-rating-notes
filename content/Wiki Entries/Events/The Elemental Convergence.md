- All four Elder Elementals meet at the center of the world at noon on one day of the year in an event called The Elemental Convergence. 
- Said day marks the exact midpoint of the year. 
- When they meet, a great but brief cataclysm occurs. Lightning, fire, wind, stone, ice, and water swirl together in a towering maelstrom that emits a blinding pillar of light. as beautiful as it is terrible, visible from almost anywhere in the world.
