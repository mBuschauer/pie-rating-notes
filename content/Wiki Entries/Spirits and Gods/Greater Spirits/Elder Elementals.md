- Comprised of the most famous [[Wiki Entries/Spirits and Gods/Great Spirits|Great Spirits]]: [[Wiki Entries/Spirits and Gods/Greater Spirits/Leviathan|Leviathan]], [[Wiki Entries/Spirits and Gods/Greater Spirits/Phoenix|Phoenix]], [[Wiki Entries/Spirits and Gods/Greater Spirits/Tempest|Tempest]], and [[Wiki Entries/Spirits and Gods/Greater Spirits/Zaratan|Zaratan]].
- Each one can dwarf an island in size if they so choose 
- Their presence directly influences the entire world’s climate and geography
- At the midpoint of the year, all four Elder Elementals meet at the center of the world in an event called [[Wiki Entries/Events/The Elemental Convergence|The Elemental Convergence]]
- At the end of each year, a night of near complete spiritual absence occurs as all the Elder Elementals must make the journey underneath the world to return to their respective starting positions.
	- The absence of the Elder Elementals seems to have effects on all spirits
