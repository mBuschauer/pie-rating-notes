- One of the [[Wiki Entries/Spirits and Gods/Great Spirits|Great Spirits]]
- Looks like a face, an eye, or a man curled in a fetal position to different people. 
	- The only commonality in these interpretations is that it always seems in some way scarred or wounded, no matter what anyone personally sees in it.
- Older sailors also say that the waves become more restless in the presence of Father Moon, saying that as his form waxes and wanes, so do the tides of the night.

- The Moon rises from the Western Edge moments after [[Wiki Entries/Spirits and Gods/Greater Spirits/The Sun|The Sun]] dips beyond [[Wiki Entries/Locations/Aecor|Aecor]]'s eastern edge, then travels back across the sky.
