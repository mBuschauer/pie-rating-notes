- Spirits are a broad class of semi-divine beings tied to particular locations, natural laws and phenomena, or monster and animal species. 
- They all share the ability to travel outside of the material world, alter their appearance and especially their size, and they can choose whether or not to be visible, invisible, corporeal, semi-corporeal, or incorporeal. 
- Most range from indifferent to aggressive in their demeanor towards humanoid species and very, very few ever choose to appear humanoid. 
- They almost never speak the language of people, and instead choose to communicate to them (if absolutely necessary) through dreams, memories, and visions. 
- Most inhabited islands will have a local Spirit that the communities living there worship and try to appease.
- [[Wiki Entries/Spirits and Gods/Great Spirits|Great Spirits]] are the highest form of Spirits
