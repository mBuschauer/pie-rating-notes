- Great Spirits are the highest form of [[Wiki Entries/Spirits and Gods/Spirits|Spirits]], presences that are vast, alien, and not truly contained to any one locale.
- Their movements are the movements of the world itself.
- The most well-known Great Spirits are the [[Wiki Entries/Spirits and Gods/Greater Spirits/Elder Elementals|Elder Elementals]]
- [[Wiki Entries/Spirits and Gods/Greater Spirits/The Sun|The Sun]] and [[Wiki Entries/Spirits and Gods/Greater Spirits/The Moon|The Moon]] are similarly important Great Spirits.  
	- While the Sun is too radiant to make out any features, the Moon looks like a face, an eye, or a man curled in a fetal position to different people. 
	- The only commonality in these interpretations is that it always seems in some way scarred or wounded, no matter what anyone personally sees in it.
