## Technology
- Ancient Arlunite technology, fueled by some source of magic that is unknown to the public, is still far ahead of anything the post-[[Wiki Entries/History/Events/The Flood|The Flood|flood]] world can produce without the help of said Arlunite tech. 
- It is sought-after, but it is not necessarily rare. Instead, it is simply very hard to get. 
	- Divers dedicate their entire lives to studying the practice of going down to the sea floor, scavenging the submerged ruins of the Old World for useful tech, and doing so while staying alive and profitable. 
	- It is a lucrative business for those skilled, well-equipped, and foolhardy enough to do it.
