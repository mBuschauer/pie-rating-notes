- Full name: Lady Isa  “The First Flame”  Zutame
- Aarakocra Druid of the Circle of Wildfire and surprisingly spiritual for someone in her line of work.
- Part of the new Coalition of [[Wiki Entries/Groups/Northern Warlords|Northern Warlords]]
- Captain of the Starthief Pirates


# History
- A ruthless warrior who took over the infamous and storied Starthief Pirates in a bloody mutiny. 
- Over several years, she took her crew to greater and greater heights, eventually claiming a substantial amount of land in the North, disrupting trade routes and sovereign states all the while.
